Theorem · Jean Taylor, 1976

For ideal area-minimizing films in 3D, interior points have only three local types: a smooth sheet, a Y junction or a T junction.

These are local rules. They do not make every dipped film a global optimizer.

This is the repository’s numerical network model, not a recording of real soap.
