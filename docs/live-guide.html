REHEARSAL / 3–4 MINUTES

Try to beat the obstruction.

  1. Prediction, 30 seconds. A four-dimensional ball and a smaller cylinder. The missing coordinate is colour. Ask whether preserving volume is enough to keep the ball out.
  2. Volume, 40 seconds. Shrink the first two coordinates and expand the other two. Four-volume stays constant, but the first conjugate area shrinks. The ball fits.
  3. Linear symplectic, 40 seconds. Squeeze one coordinate and expand its conjugate partner. The disk becomes an ellipse of the same area. The analytic projected radius decides containment.
  4. Nonlinear, 60 seconds. Press Play. These are gradient kicks and drifts, a conjugate squeeze, and symplectic rotations. They cannot embed the ball into a smaller standard conjugate cylinder. The sampled shadow is an estimate, not a theorem check.
  5. Change the plane, 40 seconds. The cylinder now constrains two position coordinates. That plane is Lagrangian. A symplectic map can shrink both positions while expanding both momenta.

The one sentence to land

Preserving symplectic geometry is stronger than preserving volume. Which pair of coordinates makes the hole matters.

Controls and reset

Left/Right or 1–5 selects a beat. Space plays or pauses. N opens narration. R restarts the current beat. F requests fullscreen where supported. Buttons remain keyboard-accessible; shortcuts do not hijack a focused slider.

Each beat resets deformation, time, trace, camera and hole radius to the rehearsal setting R/r = 0.72. Changing a slider is optional exploration. Restart beat restores that starting point.

Read the numbers correctly

“Boundary samples outside” counts points sampled on the sphere S³; it is not a fraction of four-dimensional ball volume. The shadow area is a grid estimate. The reference line is a Gromov lower bound only in the linear symplectic and Hamiltonian conjugate-plane scenes.

Containment is calculated analytically for the linear maps. For the nonlinear family, R/r < 1 is ruled out by non-squeezing. At a larger radius the sampled picture alone does not certify containment.
